# Translation and Review Notes

## Recommended final review before publication

1. **Brand voice:** confirm preferred capitalization and service naming for Inklusion in English.
2. **Disability terminology:** translations generally use “people with disabilities,” while preserving established terms from named laws, studies, and organizations.
3. **Jurisdiction:** retain every distinction between guidance that applies in Mexico and references that describe U.S., European, Ukrainian, or international frameworks.
4. **Legal review:** educational articles are not legal advice. Any article used commercially should be reviewed against the current rules in the intended market.
5. **Internal links:** translated articles point to the original Spanish public URLs so no nonexistent English web route is implied.
6. **Calls to action:** service URLs remain the current Inklusion URLs. Their destination pages may still be Spanish-language pages.
7. **Dates and figures:** all dates, statistics, currencies, names, and source claims were preserved from the Spanish originals rather than independently updated.

## Quality controls applied

- One-to-one source/translation inventory
- Full-body translation rather than summaries
- Structural comparison of headings and list items
- Word-count ratio checks for omission detection
- Source-slug and source-URL verification
- Manual review of representative long-form, legal-context, technical-accessibility, and current-events articles

## Scope date

This export covers the 34 articles present in the source repository on August 17, 2026. Articles added later are not included unless the translation workflow is rerun.
